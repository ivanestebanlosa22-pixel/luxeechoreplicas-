Quality Control (QC): The Most Important Step

QC photos are taken by the agent before shipping. This is your only chance to approve or reject the product. Think of it as a pre-shipment inspection that protects your money.

What to Check in QC Photos

  1. Shape and proportions - Does the silhouette match retail?
  2. Stitching and alignment - Are lines straight and even?
  3. Colors - Compare with retail reference photos
  4. Logos and text - Check spelling, positioning, and size
  5. Materials - Does the texture look right?
  6. Overall symmetry - Left and right should match

⚠ Important

If something looks wrong in QC photos, reject the item. Rejecting QC does not penalize your account. The agent will either get a replacement from the seller or refund you. Never approve QC photos that show obvious flaws.

5

Customs: What Really Happens

Customs processes thousands of packages daily. They do not target individual buyers. Most packages pass through without any issues when shipped correctly.

Possible Outcomes if Stopped

  • Released with delay - Most common. Package arrives a few days late.
  • Taxed - You pay import duties. Varies by country.
  • Destruction - Item is destroyed. No further consequences in most countries.

In most countries, buyers are not fined or prosecuted for personal-use replicas. The risk is losing the item and the money you paid, which is why using a trusted agent with QC is essential.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Buying too fast - Always research the seller and read community reviews before ordering.
  2. Ignoring QC photos - Never skip QC review. This is your best protection against bad quality.
  3. Choosing the cheapest shipping - Budget shipping has higher seizure risk. Use Special Line.
  4. Buying high-risk items first - Start with sneakers and hoodies. Watches and bags have higher customs risk.
  5. Trusting TikTok shops - Instagram and TikTok sellers often use stolen photos. Always use verified agents.
  6. Not consolidating orders - Ship multiple items together to save on international shipping costs.
  7. Expecting 1:1 retail quality - AAA+ replicas are extremely close, but minor differences may exist. Manage expectations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is buying replicas illegal?

Buying replicas for personal use is not a crime in most countries. Selling them as authentic is illegal. Customs may seize items, but buyers are typically not fined or prosecuted.

Can my package be seized by customs?

Yes, but it's rare when using proper shipping methods (Special Line or Tax-Free Line). Seized packages are usually destroyed without further consequences to the buyer.

How long does shipping take?

Europe: 8-20 days. USA: 7-18 days. Latin America: 12-30 days. Times vary by shipping method and current conditions.

Can I return items?

You cannot return items like on Amazon, but you can reject items during QC before they ship. This is actually better - you check quality through photos before the item ever leaves China.

What payment methods do agents accept?

Most agents accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, Alipay, and some accept cryptocurrency. All payments are secure and processed through the agent platform.
