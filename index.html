FAQ

What is the best buying agent for replicas?
It depends on your region. USFans is excellent for the US ($800 bonus, English support). Kakobuy is the all-rounder with the lowest fees and a huge user base. Litbuy is great for Europe with €/QC support and welcome coupons.
Are replicas good quality?
Quality varies by batch and seller. We focus on AAA+ verified items with real QC photos so you can approve the exact product before it ships from China.
How long does shipping take?
Typically 7-15 business days via express lines with full tracking. Your agent inspects items and sends QC photos before shipping internationally.
How much can I save versus retail?
Most buyers save 60-80% versus retail. A $200 retail product often costs $30-60 including agent fees and shipping.
Is it safe to order through an agent?
Yes. A reputable agent buys on your behalf, inspects with QC photos and ships with buyer protection and tracking — removing most of the risk.
