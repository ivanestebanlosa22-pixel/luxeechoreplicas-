Which Jordan 1 batch is best?

How much do Jordan 1 replicas cost?
Most AAA+ Jordan 1 replicas range from about $35 to $75 including the shoe, agent fee and shipping — roughly 70-80% less than retail.
Are Jordan 1 reps true to size?
Most batches fit true to size, but some run slightly large. Check the QC photos and the sizing notes on the product page, or ask the community before ordering.
