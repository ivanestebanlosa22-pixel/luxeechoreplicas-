Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping from China takes 7-15 business days. Europe: 8-20 days, USA: 7-18 days, Latin America: 12-30 days. Special Line shipping is recommended for the safest delivery. See our shipping guide for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are processed through our verified shopping agents (USFans, Litbuy, Kakobuy). They accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, and cryptocurrency. All transactions are secure with buyer protection.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, the agent provides a tracking number. You can track your package directly on the agent's platform or through the shipping carrier's website (EMS, DHL, etc.).

What are QC photos?

QC (Quality Control) photos are taken by the agent at their warehouse before shipping. They show the actual product you'll receive from multiple angles. You can approve or reject the item based on these photos. Read our QC guide for more information.

Can I return items?

Returns work differently than regular online stores. You cannot return items for a refund, but you can reject items during QC review before they ship. This is actually better - you verify quality through photos before the item ever leaves China.
