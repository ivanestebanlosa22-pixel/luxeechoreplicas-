How to QC Designer Bag Replicas

When you receive QC photos from your agent, check these elements:

Exterior

  • Pattern alignment: Monogram/canvas pattern should align at seams (not cut mid-logo)
  • Hardware finish: Should be shiny, not dull or flaky. Check engraving depth
  • Stitching: Even pitch, consistent color, no loose threads
  • Leather/canvas: Consistent texture, no bubbles or peeling

Interior

  • Lining color should match the authentic reference
  • Interior pocket placement and zipper quality
  • Heat stamp or serial number stamp depth and font
  • Dustbag color and logo accuracy
Red flags to reject: Misaligned pattern at corners, flaking hardware, off-color lining, visible glue residue, loose stitching at handles.
